From a Diner Booth to a $5 Trillion Empire:

How Nvidia Changed the Future of Technology

How a Gaming Company Became the World's AI Powerhouse

When people hear the name NVIDIA, they often think of gaming graphics cards. But today, NVIDIA is much more
than a gaming company. It has become the backbone of artificial intelligence(AI),powering everything from ChatGPT
to self-driving cars and advanced data centers.

1.The Beginning

NVIDIA was founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. Their vision was simple
but ambitious: create powerful graphics processors that could deliver realistic computer graphics.

In 1999, NVIDIA launched the GeForce 256, widely recognized as the world's first Graphics Processing Unit (GPU).
This product changed PC gaming forever.

2.Beyond Gaming

For years, gamers were NVIDIA's biggest customers. However, the company realized that GPUs could do
much more than render graphics.
GPUs are designed to handle multiple tasks simultaneously, making them ideal for parallel processing.
Unlike traditional CPUs, GPUs can perform thousands of calculations simultaneously. This makes them ideal for:
Artificial Intelligence Scientific research Machine learning Cryptocurrency mining High-performance computing
This shift became one of the smartest strategic decisions in technology history.

3.The AI Revolution

Artificial intelligence requires enormous computing power to train large language models.
Companies such as OpenAI, Microsoft, Meta, Google, Amazon, and many startups rely heavily on NVIDIA GPUs for AI training.
Its flagship AI chips, including the H100 and Blackwell platforms, have become the industry's gold standard.
Demand became so high that many companies waited months to receive their orders.
